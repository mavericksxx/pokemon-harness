---
title: Multi-workspace
order: 14
---

The app can run more than one garden at once, each pointed at its own
project folder with its own independent set of sessions. Switch between them
from the topbar's garden picker, or directly with Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+1 through 9
(in registry order) without opening any menu at all. Live-session counts,
the quit dialog, and keep-awake already span every open garden rather than
just the active one.
