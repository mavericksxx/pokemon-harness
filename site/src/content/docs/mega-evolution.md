---
title: Mega evolution
order: 9
---

46 species (48 forms — Charizard and Mewtwo each carry a Mega X and a Mega
Y) mega-evolve automatically when they're the parent walker in a battle (see
[Subagent battles](/docs/subagent-battles/)): no picker entry, no manual
toggle, no work-based threshold — being in the mega-forms table is the only
trigger. It's the complete classic, pre-Legends Z-A official set; the
roughly two dozen Mega Evolutions Pokémon Legends: Z-A added (Dragonite,
Victreebel, Clefable, and the rest) aren't in yet. A species with two forms
picks X or Y deterministically, hashed off the battle itself, so the same
battle always shows the same form and different battles alternate over time
— never a random re-roll mid-fight.

The ceremony plays out in stages — orbs and a dim-down into silhouette, an
energy ring with twin flame plumes, a vortex pull-in, a flash at the hot
core, then the reveal — holding the battle's current wave for its roughly
3.6-second runtime, with a floating "Mega Evolved!" once it lands. The form
reverts the instant the battle's forced stance releases.
