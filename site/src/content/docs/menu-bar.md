---
title: Menu bar
order: 12
---

A pokéball glyph sits in the macOS menu bar for as long as the app is
running, independent of the main window — closing or hiding the window
doesn't take it down. Clicking it opens a small popover (its own frameless
window, not a native menu, since progress bars and a sparkline don't fit
inside one) with three read-only sections: provider usage limits (see
[Usage tracking](/docs/usage-tracking/)), the 30-day cost-history sparkline
(see [Cost tracking](/docs/cost-tracking/)), and live counts of working,
idle, and needs-you sessions across every workspace.
