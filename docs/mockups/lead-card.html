A lead must read as clearly below Arceus, and no treatment may introduce a second accent colour — everything below is built from gold, the existing grounds, and the existing text tokens only.

A hairline gold left edge + pill

Signals: "elevated" without the full Arceus frame — a rank bar is a familiar low-intensity emphasis idiom, and it costs almost no space in the rail.

Weakness: this is the wrong slot. AgentRosterCard.tsx already colors every ordinary card's own 3px left border from session.accent — one of six existing hues, one of which (lemon) sits a few degrees from gold itself. A gold left edge occupies the exact same visual position every other card already uses for an unrelated per-session color, so at a glance a lead risks reading as "a session with an odd accent" rather than "elevated," especially next to a lemon-accented ordinary card.

B corner accents, no left bar

Signals: frees the left edge for its existing job (session accent), and corner marks read as "framed" without claiming the whole card border.

Weakness: corner accents are Arceus's own signature idiom (.roster-card-arceus-corner, four of them, with a stepped shadow). Two lighter, shadow-less corners on a lead card still visually quotes that exact device — the one thing §3.9 explicitly asks this treatment NOT to do is echo Arceus's frame. Even at reduced intensity, a user who has learned "corners = Arceus" may misread this as a diminished Arceus moment rather than a distinct rank.

C gold kicker underline (proposed)

Signals: a 2px gold rule under the kicker row is a new axis nothing else in the card grammar currently uses — not the left-border slot (session accent), not the corner slot (Arceus). It reads as "this row is capped/ranked" without competing with either existing signal, and like A/B it pairs with the mono lead pill so the badge, not just a stray line, carries the actual word.

Weakness: it is the subtlest of the three. A 2px horizontal rule sits close in weight to the card's own 1px --line border and to dividers elsewhere in the rail; at the rail's default width, a user skimming quickly may register the pill but miss the rule entirely, which means this treatment leans on the pill more than A or B do rather than truly having two independent signals.

what stayed constant across all three

  • Every gold used is one of the app's two real gold values (--gold / --accent-text) — no new hue anywhere, dark or light.
  • radius: 0 on every rect; the only two shadows in the whole page are Arceus's own pre-existing hard offsets (no blur), reused unchanged.
  • Press Start 2P appears only at 12px (page title) — never inside a card, matching tokens.ts's note that Press Start 2P is chrome-only and breaks below/between its three fixed sizes.
  • None of A/B/C touch Arceus's bezel, crest, corners-with-shadow, or ceremonial padding — each sits on the plain .roster-card-medium shell every ordinary session card already uses.
